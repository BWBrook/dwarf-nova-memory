# Writing notes for the prose rewrite

These are Barry's instructions, given during the reconstruction on 10 November 2026. Apply them when
the products are redrafted.

1. **Write for a new reader.** Assume nothing: name what SS Cygni is, what a dwarf nova does and what
   a forecast score measures before using them. Too much wood for trees is the current failure.
2. **State findings plainly.** A main message or headline carries no uncertainties or caveats.
3. **Stop attaching negations to positive statements.** Avoid "X, not Y", "X, but this does not
   establish Z" and a following sentence that walks the claim back. The current drafts do this
   throughout.
4. **Gather constraints, assumptions and limitations in one place:** a dedicated limitations
   paragraph or section, instead of injecting them repeatedly through the text.
   - The estimands still apply. Every qualification they require must appear somewhere, normally in
     that section, and a positive statement must stay accurate on its own terms.
   - For example: "duration raised the working score" stays, with the meaning of the working score
     explained where the score is defined and its limits stated in the limitations section.
   - Two estimands rules conflict with this and must be revised before the redraft: "Put this
     qualification beside the headline" (D047) and "Conditions beside the headline" (C01 section).
     Barry's instruction takes precedence.
