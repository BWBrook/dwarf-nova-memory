# Step 1b: abstract (under 150 words)

Reconciled with Codex's proposal (`06-codex-review.md` §4), 10 November 2026; dispositions in
`08-reconciliation.md`.

> Dwarf novae brighten in repeated outbursts when the gas disc around a white dwarf heats up. In SS
> Cygni, longer outbursts tend to precede longer quiet intervals. Using a century of amateur
> observations from the AAVSO, we tested whether outburst duration improves forecasts of the next
> onset beyond recent timing. Lognormal recurrence models fitted on 1910–1959 were frozen and
> preregistered before 1960–2017 was opened. Duration raised the working log score by 0.0165 nats
> per forecast (90% interval 0.0049–0.0281; 441 forecasts), meeting the preregistered criterion. Two
> redesigned timings and both preregistered secondary tests gave smaller gains with intervals
> spanning zero. A frozen screen of ten other dwarf novae found none whose pre-1990 records could
> repeat the test. Linking the working score to physical onsets requires a validated observation
> model, and shared end estimates or activity-dependent observing could couple duration with timing.
> A documented parser repair followed first data access.

## Choices

- **Structure:** context, question, design, result, checks, transfer, then one closing passage of
  qualifications, as the revised estimands rule allows for a standalone abstract. No qualification
  is attached to a result sentence.
- **The question is incremental** ("beyond recent timing"), because a held-out onset forecast
  already exists in the literature, for a different star and without duration (C41).
- **Kept from Codex's version:** the incremental question, the secondaries reported as results, and
  the compact closing qualifications, including the parser repair (C05), which the estimands require
  wherever the primary result is reported.
- **Kept from Claude's version:** the century of amateur observations, which is part of why the
  result matters, and the observed direction in SS Cyg as motivation.
- **Left out:** the 1.7% translation (C02), the 0.117-nat timing-baseline scale (C10), the block
  count (C25) and the 1980s yield ratios (C70). Each belongs in the results.
- **OJAp's abstract limit:** none is stated (`docs/audits/2026-11-10-ojap-style-comparison.md`). The
  five comparison papers ran 97–245 words.
