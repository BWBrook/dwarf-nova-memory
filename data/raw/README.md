# Immutable raw acquisitions

No astronomical light curves have been acquired for this seed. Put original
exports here without editing them. They are ignored by Git. Record every export
in `data/manifests/acquisitions.csv`, including its SHA-256 hash, query, source,
time and passband documentation, access date and redistribution terms. Preserve
native error, flag, upper-limit, observer, instrument and availability fields.

Public availability does not automatically authorize republishing an entire
database. Verify the applicable archive terms before depositing measurements.
