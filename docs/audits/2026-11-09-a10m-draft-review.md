memo. It source-checked the full V363 Lyr forecasting PDF and the Price preprint,
and screened all 68 citing titles/identifiers returned by the specified CM92

test allocation and point-error scoring. The paper reports no preregistration,
scored predictive onset distribution or explicit completed-duration addition
against a common timing baseline. This is a check of the reported design, not
a full leakage audit of its preprocessing or code. A10l's categorical wording
that nothing was probabilistic should be read as “no scored predictive onset
distribution is reported”; random fitting runs are a different object.

supported as an account of this bounded independent search.** It does not
certify universal priority. ADS access failed; observer/non-English coverage
is incomplete, and some full texts, including Tsuranova et al. (2026), remain

must stay. The original historical-source check C40 is retained; this turn
