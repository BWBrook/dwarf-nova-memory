# Blind independent 1957 event annotation

## Scientific conclusion

Applying A04-v1 to the supplied 1957 subset produced eight adjudicated events: six confirmed normal events with unambiguous brackets, one confirmed event with an ambiguous threshold-tail end, and one unconfirmed event whose end is censored by the subset boundary. Three events are long, four are short, and the right-edge-censored unconfirmed event is type-ambiguous. Four >4.0 d gaps between consecutive informative rows make the first three following-event pairs unverified as consecutive; the other within-subset pairs have no protocol-defined coverage gap.

This is an event-bracketing annotation, not evidence of physical memory. I did not calculate a duration–following-gap correlation, forecast, model, effect size, or physical interpretation.

## Mechanical derivation and adjudication

The independent implementation produced 20 maximal mechanical B runs and four coverage gaps. Every candidate, gap, and T row was reviewed from ID-keyed local listings.

- Two isolated threshold B runs, M05 and M06, were dropped. B IDs `50674949` and `50674951` were contradicted by F IDs `50674950` and `50674952`.
- M08–M11 were merged into one event across isolated F IDs `50675233`, `50675238`, and `50675305`, each contradicted by B reports within 0.5 d. Isolated tail B IDs `50675310`, `50675315`, and `50675317` were dropped because multiple nearby F rows contradicted them.
- M15 is the strict primary form of E1957-07. M16–M19 were not accepted as separate events. They instead define an alternative threshold-tail end because two F rows at JD 2436167.5 prevent the protocol-authorized isolated-F merge. The primary end is `[2436167.239, 2436167.5)`; the recorded alternative is `[2436167.7, 2436168.29)`.
- M20 is retained as an unconfirmed, right-edge-censored event. Its two B rows share one observer; no observation outside the supplied subset was used.

The four reviewed coverage gaps are bounded by native ID pairs `50674520`–`50674521`, `50674549`–`50674550`, `50674556`–`50674557`, and `50674575`–`50674576`. They occur in the quiescent intervals following E1957-01, E1957-02 (two gaps), and E1957-03, respectively.

Including T rows would not alter any retained primary onset or end bracket. Several excluded faint T rows would mechanically split otherwise well-supported B runs, and excluded bright T ID `50675467` would create an isolated unconfirmed run between E1957-07 and E1957-08. These effects are recorded in `decisions.csv`; T rows remain excluded from primary decisions.

## Protocol objections and limitations

1. The mechanical definition fragments a single threshold crossing whenever an F row occurs, including at identical JDs. The permitted merge rule covers an isolated contradictory F row but not multiple mutually inconsistent B and F rows at the same epoch. E1957-07 therefore requires explicit primary and alternative end brackets.
2. “Mark any boundary ambiguous” has no prescribed output representation and does not say which alternative controls duration, confirmation, or following-gap calculations. I retained the strict mechanical bracket as primary, stored the threshold-tail bracket separately, and used the primary bracket for derived columns.
3. “Including T rows would change the bracket” does not cover a T row that creates an additional candidate or splits an event without changing its outer boundary. `decisions.csv` distinguishes these structural effects from boundary effects.
4. “Near-peak” in the slow-rise judgement has no numerical tolerance. Slow-rise notes are therefore qualitative; no event classification depends on them.
5. The meanings of the non-current comment codes are not supplied. They were preserved and noted where a boundary or edge event rests on one, but not interpreted.
6. Consecutiveness is only the protocol's conditional check for >4.0 d gaps between informative rows. Dense observation times do not establish complete detection, and irregular observation may respond to brightness.

No external astronomy source, repository file, existing candidate/annotation code, prior result, or outside-subset observation was read.
