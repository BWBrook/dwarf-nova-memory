# Suggested first task for Codex: reproducibility and one authentic light curve

Read AGENTS.md, README, methods and data contracts. Claim a bounded task in the
board. The project's principal output will be honest forecast evaluation; a
large codebase or a passing toy demonstration is not the scientific goal.

1. Run the seed checks. Independently verify a survival/interval probability and
   inspect the chronological split. Identify implemented versus planned features.
   Report concrete bugs or misleading scope; do not manufacture findings.
2. Use the documented archive route for one provisional ordinary dwarf nova.
   Obtain a modest authentic export with authorisation already available to the
   user. If a login/export is needed and no supported path is available, report
   the exact requirement and continue useful local work; do not invent an API.
3. Preserve the raw bytes, hash, query, source/version, retrieval date, time/band
   semantics and applicable reuse terms. Populate the acquisition manifest.
4. Build a narrow adapter with a small lawful fixture. Quarantine unknown time
   conventions and preserve limits, flags and observer/instrument codes. Test
   the consequential conversions and duplicate handling.
5. Produce a by-band, by-season quality/coverage plot and a concise audit. Do not
   combine visual, V and survey bands into one fluence series without evidence.
6. Exchange a small development interval with the other agent for independent
   event annotation. Only then design the real event extraction/coverage rule.

Save code, a data audit and a handoff containing commands, observed results and
limitations. Do not fit the complete-oracle demonstration directly to sparse
real detections. Do not choose the final sample using future resolved gaps or
pleasant-looking correlations. Follow the roadmap instead of implementing every
latent model at once.
